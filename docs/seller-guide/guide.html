Seller guide

A Set up your phone (one time, 3 minutes)

1Open and log in

Open Chrome. Go to getgoat.vercel.app/seller. Enter your email and password.

2Install the app

Tap ⋮ (top right) → Install app or Add to Home screen. Open it from the new goat icon.

3Turn on alerts

On the seller home tap Turn on phone alerts, then Allow.

4Check the test alert

A test alert comes in seconds. Real sales look like the lower one. Tap it to open the order.

B How a sale works

YOU1. Add a goat

+ Add goat → details, price, tag → Save. Then add up to 8 photos and 1 short video. Does: pregnant / kids born.

YOU2. Share on WhatsApp

Tap Share on one goat, or Share all goats. Choose the chat. The buyer sees photo, name and price.

BUYER3. Buyer pays

Taps Buy this goat, gives name, ID, cellphone and address, pays with Payfast. The goat is held for 5 minutes, then shows SOLD.

APP4. You get an alert

Phone alert + red number on the bell. The order is under Paid – to release.

YOU5. Receipt + document

Open the order. Tap Send receipt (WhatsApp to buyer). Tap Removal document (PDF) and print it.

YOU6. Collection

Fill in the seller section. You and the buyer (or driver) sign. Check the tag. Tap Mark as collected.

Good to know

  • Edit a goat: tap it under My goats. Change price, photos (tap Cover for the main photo) or details.
  • Not for sale now? Hide from marketplace. Delete needs two taps.
  • Sold goats stay visible with a SOLD stamp for 14 days. Buyers see that you sell.
  • Goat without a photo shows Add photo instead of Share. Add a photo first.
  • Buyer has a question? The goat page has Ask a question and Call – they reach 083 442 6012.
  • Receipt link always works. The buyer can open it any time.
PAID = release goat COLLECTED = done REFUND NEEDED

Rules and safety

  • Release a goat only when the order shows PAID in the app. Never on a screenshot alone.
  • REFUND NEEDED (rare): two buyers paid for one goat. Refund the second buyer in Payfast, then tap Refund done.
  • Keep your copy of the signed removal document. The buyer keeps theirs for at least 1 year (Stock Theft Act).
  • Check if the destination needs a veterinary movement permit.
  • ID numbers are private. Share them only on the removal document.
  • 8 wrong passwords lock login for 15 minutes.
  • Alerts stopped? Open the app → Turn on phone alerts again, or tap Test.
